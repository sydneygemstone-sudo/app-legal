# Privacy Policy — Welcome Book

**Effective date: 29 May 2026**

Last updated: 29 May 2026

---

## At a glance

Welcome Book helps you create guest welcome guides for your rental properties. Most data stays on your phone — property details, photos, and settings are stored locally. The only things that touch the internet are: (1) publishing your welcome book to a shareable web page, (2) location data used to show nearby places on a map, and (3) the App Store purchase system. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

---

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase history & subscription status** | Manage your Pro subscription | RevenueCat (anonymous user ID, no name or email) |
| **Device push notification token** | Send publishing and property alerts | Apple Push Notification service (encrypted in transit) |
| **Camera / photo library access** | Add property and room photos to your welcome book | Photos stored locally; published photos appear on the guest web page |
| **Location data** | Show your property on a map and display nearby amenities | Apple Maps / map tiles (your property's approximate location is sent to display the map) |
| **Clipboard access** | Copy shareable links and passwords to clipboard | Stays on your device |
| **Netlify access token** | Publish your welcome book to a shareable web page | Stored securely on your device (expo-secure-store); used to deploy pages via Netlify |

### Published content

When you publish a welcome book, the following content becomes available on a public web page:

- Property name and description
- Wi-Fi network name and password (you choose what to include)
- Check-in/check-out instructions
- House rules
- Local recommendations and tips
- Property photos
- Map with nearby amenities

This content is accessible to anyone with the link or QR code you share. You can unpublish at any time from within the app.

---

## What we DON'T collect

- **No personal information** — no name, email, phone number required to use the app
- **No account or sign-up** — Welcome Book has no user accounts
- **No analytics or tracking** — no Firebase, Google Analytics, Amplitude, or any tracking SDK
- **No crash reporting** — no Sentry or similar services
- **No guest data** — guests view your welcome book in their browser; we don't track who views it
- **No ads** — no ad networks, no ad tracking identifiers

---

## AI features

Welcome Book may offer AI-assisted content suggestions to help you write property descriptions and local tips faster. If used, this AI processing happens on your device. No property details or generated content is sent to external AI servers.

## Your rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of data held about you (limited to RevenueCat's anonymous purchase record)
- **Deletion**: Uninstalling the app deletes local data. You can unpublish web pages from within the app. Contact us to delete RevenueCat records.
- **Portability**: Export your property data from within the app
- **Correction**: Edit any property details directly in the app and republish

---

## Children's privacy

Welcome Book is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

---

## Guest privacy

Your guests access the welcome book through a public link or QR code. The web page does not:

- Track visitors or collect analytics
- Require guests to sign up or provide any information
- Use cookies or tracking pixels
- Share visitor data with third parties

Guests can view the page and leave. That's it.

---

## Data retention

- **Local property data**: Remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data
- **Published web pages**: Available until you unpublish them from within the app
- **RevenueCat records**: Retained while your subscription is active, then per RevenueCat's retention policy
- **Push notification tokens**: Removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the app
