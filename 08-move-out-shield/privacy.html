Rotamatic — Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 13, 2026

Rotamatic ("we", "our", "the app") is published by Chen Fu. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Shift data you enter — shift times, labels, notes, templates, and pay rates. This data is stored only on your device using local storage (MMKV). We do not upload it to any server.

Photos you take for roster scanning — processed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. Photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored beyond the scanning session.

Anonymous usage analytics — we collect aggregated, non-identifying usage events (e.g. "user opened calendar", "user scanned a roster") via PostHog to improve the app. These events contain no personal data, shift content, or device identifiers that can be traced back to you.

Subscription status — managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your anonymous App Store subscriber ID to validate your subscription. We do not see your Apple ID, name, or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your location (GPS or otherwise)
  • Health data (we do not use HealthKit)
  • Contacts, messages, or call history
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Photos from your camera roll (roster photos are processed in-memory and discarded)

3. Calendar Access

If you enable "Sync to iOS Calendar", Rotamatic writes shift events to a dedicated calendar on your device using Apple's EventKit framework. This data stays on your device and syncs only through your own iCloud account, which we have no access to. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Notifications

If you enable shift reminders, Rotamatic schedules local notifications on your device. These do not involve any server. No push notification tokens are generated or transmitted.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All shift data is stored locally. To delete it, go to Settings → Clear all shifts, or simply delete the app. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data.

7. Children's Privacy

Rotamatic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
