# Privacy Policy — Keepsie

**Effective date: 29 May 2026**

Last updated: 29 May 2026

---

## At a glance

Keepsie keeps your purchase records on your phone. Scan a receipt, track warranty dates, log what you own — all without sending anything to the cloud. No account, no server, no tracking. The only internet connection is for managing your Pro subscription.

---

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase history & subscription status** | Manage your Pro subscription | RevenueCat (anonymous user ID, no name or email) |
| **Device push notification token** | Send warranty and return deadline reminders | Apple Push Notification service (encrypted in transit) |
| **Camera access** | Scan receipts for item entry | Photo processed on-device by AI, never uploaded |
| **Microphone access** | Voice input for adding items | Audio processed on-device, never recorded or uploaded |

All other data — your items, receipt details, warranty dates, categories, photos, and settings — is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (MMKV). It never leaves your phone.

---

## What we DON'T collect

- **No personal information** — no name, email, phone number, or address
- **No account or sign-up** — Keepsie has no user accounts
- **No analytics or tracking** — no Firebase, Google Analytics, Amplitude, or any tracking SDK
- **No crash reporting** — no Sentry or similar services
- **No location data** — Keepsie doesn't know where you shop
- **No financial data on servers** — your purchase amounts and warranty info stay on your device
- **No ads** — no ad networks, no ad tracking identifiers

---

## AI features

Keepsie uses on-device AI to:

- Read and extract information from receipt photos (product name, price, store, date)
- Process voice input when you describe a purchase

All AI processing happens on your device. No receipt images, audio, or purchase data is sent to a server or cloud API.

---

## Your rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of data held about you (limited to RevenueCat's anonymous purchase record)
- **Deletion**: Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. Contact us to delete RevenueCat records.
- **Portability**: Export your item data from within the app (Pro feature)
- **Correction**: Edit any item, receipt, or warranty date directly in the app

---

## Children's privacy

Keepsie is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

---

## Data retention

- **Local item data**: Remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data
- **RevenueCat records**: Retained while your subscription is active, then per RevenueCat's retention policy
- **Push notification tokens**: Removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the app

---
