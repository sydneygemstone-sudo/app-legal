# Privacy Policy — PawLog Pet Care

**Effective date: 29 May 2026**

Last updated: 29 May 2026

---

## At a glance

PawLog keeps your pet's health records on your phone. Vaccine history, feeding logs, walk schedules — everything stays local. No cloud sync, no user accounts, no tracking. The only internet connection is for managing your Pro subscription through Apple.

---

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase history & subscription status** | Manage your Pro subscription | RevenueCat (anonymous user ID, no name or email) |
| **Device push notification token** | Send vaccine booster reminders and daily care alerts | Apple Push Notification service (encrypted in transit) |
| **Calendar access** | Add vaccine booster dates to your iOS Calendar | Stays in your local Calendar app |

All other data — pet profiles, vaccine records, feeding logs, activity history, reminders, and settings — is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (MMKV). It never leaves your phone.

---

## What we DON'T collect

- **No personal information** — no name, email, phone number, or address
- **No account or sign-up** — PawLog has no user accounts
- **No analytics or tracking** — no Firebase, Google Analytics, Amplitude, or any tracking SDK
- **No crash reporting** — no Sentry or similar services
- **No location data** — PawLog doesn't track where you or your pets go
- **No camera or microphone access** — PawLog doesn't use either
- **No pet health data on servers** — your pet's records stay on your device
- **No ads** — no ad networks, no ad tracking identifiers

---

## Your rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of data held about you (limited to RevenueCat's anonymous purchase record)
- **Deletion**: Uninstalling the app deletes all local pet data. Contact us to delete RevenueCat records.
- **Portability**: Export your pet's records from within the app (Pro feature)
- **Correction**: Edit any pet profile, vaccine record, or activity entry directly in the app

---

## Children's privacy

PawLog is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

---

## Data retention

- **Local pet data**: Remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data
- **RevenueCat records**: Retained while your subscription is active, then per RevenueCat's retention policy
- **Push notification tokens**: Removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the app

---

## Changes to this policy

If we change how PawLog handles data, this page will be updated with a new "Effective date." For significant changes, we'll show an in-app notification before they take effect.
