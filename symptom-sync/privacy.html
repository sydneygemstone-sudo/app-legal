# Privacy Policy — SymptomSync

**Effective date: 29 May 2026**

Last updated: 29 May 2026

---

## At a glance

SymptomSync is designed to be the most private health tracker you can use. Your symptoms, mood, energy, and triggers never leave your phone. There is no cloud, no account, no server. We literally cannot see your data. The only thing that touches the internet is the App Store purchase system (handled by Apple and RevenueCat).

---

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase history & subscription status** | Manage your Pro subscription | RevenueCat (anonymous user ID, no name or email) |
| **Device push notification token** | Send you daily logging reminders | Apple Push Notification service (encrypted in transit) |

That's it. Everything else — your symptoms, mood scores, energy levels, triggers, templates, trends, and exports — is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (MMKV). It never leaves your phone.

---

## What we DON'T collect

- **No health data on servers** — your symptoms, triggers, and patterns never leave your device
- **No personal information** — no name, email, phone number, or date of birth
- **No account or sign-up** — SymptomSync has no user accounts
- **No analytics or tracking** — no Firebase, Google Analytics, Amplitude, or any tracking SDK
- **No crash reporting** — no Sentry or similar services
- **No location data**
- **No camera or microphone access** — SymptomSync doesn't use either
- **No ads** — no ad networks, no ad tracking identifiers

---

## Health data specifics

SymptomSync stores the following data **entirely on your device**:

- Symptom entries (type, severity, duration, notes)
- Mood and energy scores
- Trigger associations
- Trend analysis results
- Condition templates and customisations
- Exported reports

This data is stored in MMKV (fast, encrypted local storage). It is never synced, backed up to any cloud, or accessible to anyone but you. If you delete the app, all health data is permanently deleted.

**Important**: If you use iCloud or other device backup services, your app data may be included in those backups. This is controlled by your device settings, not by SymptomSync.

## Your rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of any data held about you (limited to RevenueCat's anonymous purchase record)
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data. Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app. Contact us to delete RevenueCat records.
- **Portability**: Export your symptom data as CSV from within the app at any time (Settings → Export Data)
- **Correction**: Edit any symptom entry directly in the app

Since SymptomSync stores everything locally, you have complete control. No one — including me — can access your health data.

---

## Children's privacy

SymptomSync is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

---

## Data retention

- **Local health data**: Remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data
- **RevenueCat records**: Retained while your subscription is active, then deleted per RevenueCat's retention policy
- **Push notification tokens**: Removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the app

---

## Not medical advice disclaimer

SymptomSync is a tracking and pattern-discovery tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. The insights it shows are statistical correlations, not clinical conclusions. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical decisions.
